Can language models paint famous artworks from memory, using only text?

Scale often leads to unexpected emergent abilities in language models, so called "big model smell". This is difficult to measure, and good metrics (like the [pelican on a bicycle svg](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Oct/25/pelicans-on-a-bicycle/)) inevitably become a target.
